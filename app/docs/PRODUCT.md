# EduAI Prism Teacher Workspace

## Product

EduAI Prism is a private K-12 school AI workspace. The teacher side brings daily teaching, class insight, school knowledge, lesson research, courseware production, and AI assistance into one authenticated product.

## Primary Users

- Subject teachers preparing lessons between classes or at a school workstation.
- Teaching researchers coordinating lesson study and shared resources.
- School staff who need trustworthy, attributable information rather than decorative dashboards.

## Core Jobs

1. See what needs attention now without reading a wall of cards.
2. Move from a reliable source or class signal into an actionable teaching task.
3. Review and correct AI output before it becomes a teaching artifact.
4. Understand loading, empty, error, permission, conflict, and completion states at a glance.

## Personality

Calm, capable, warm, and precise. The teacher workspace should feel like a well-made professional instrument with a small amount of friendly physicality, not a marketing page or a children's game.

## Design Principles

1. Task first: primary actions and current status are visible before secondary metrics.
2. Evidence over decoration: every metric, recommendation, and success state must have a real source.
3. One shared language: navigation, page headers, panels, controls, and feedback states use the same hierarchy across teacher pages.
4. Calm depth: depth comes from tonal layers, restrained borders, and diffuse shadows, not glass everywhere or dramatic gradients.
5. Honest states: loading, empty, unavailable, error, conflict, and success are explicit and actionable.
6. Accessible by default: WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, and at least 44px key touch targets.

## Anti-References

- Oversized marketing heroes inside operational pages.
- Purple neon AI styling, floating decorative blobs, and glass on every surface.
- Toy-like 3D controls in serious teacher data or governance workflows.
- Nested cards, generic card walls, and multiple competing primary actions.
- Success messages before the authoritative server result has returned.
