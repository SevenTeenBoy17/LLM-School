# Product

## Register
product

## Users and Purpose
Chinese K-12 teachers preparing between classes and reviewing classroom materials in a bright office. Implement the eight approved 20260905 blueprints, preserving the existing left navigation and global shell. Shared lesson preparation centers on a concrete question, discussion, classroom observations and improvements. Artifacts center on finding, adapting and reviewing material before sharing.

## Principles
Trustworthy, practical, calm. Do not equate long AI replies with validated teaching material. Student-facing text and teacher notes remain separate. New frontend workflows use visibly labelled local sample data; real archives and template access remain available. No server writes for sample actions, no invented model progress or online collaborators. Accessibility includes keyboard navigation, reduced motion, visible focus and responsive panels.

## Anti-references
No marketing heroes, decorative metric cards, saturated purple gradients, stacked cards, floating decorative robots or ornate controls. Follow the approved document/table layouts, not generic AI dashboard templates.
